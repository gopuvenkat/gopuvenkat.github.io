I am an incoming PhD student in the
[Konrad Zuse School of Excellence in Learning and Intelligent Systems
(ELIZA)](https://eliza.school/), joining the
[Ubiquitous Knowledge Processing (UKP) Lab](https://www.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/ukp/ukp_home/index.en.jsp)
at TU Darmstadt. I will be supervised by
[Prof. Dr. Iryna Gurevych](https://www.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/ukp/ukp_home/head_ukp/index.en.jsp)
and co-supervised by [Prof. Valentin Hofmann](https://valentinhofmann.github.io/).

My research interests lie in **natural language processing** and **machine
learning**, with a particular focus on low-resource NLP, lexical semantics,
representation learning, and language models. My previous research includes
work on distributional thesauri, contextualized representations, hypernymy
detection, semantic resources, and neural models for biological sequence
prediction.

My industry experience includes work at Oracle on AI systems for translating
natural-language requests into declarative queries, including SQL, and on the
Essbase calculation engine. I completed an integrated Bachelor and Master of
Technology in Computer Science and Engineering at
[IIIT Bangalore](https://www.iiitb.ac.in/), where my master's thesis explored
enriching word representations using distributional thesauri.
