Gopalakrishnan Venkatesh

Incoming PhD Student · UKP Lab · TU Darmstadt

I am an incoming PhD student in the Konrad Zuse School of Excellence in Learning and Intelligent Systems (ELIZA) and will join the Ubiquitous Knowledge Processing (UKP) Lab at TU Darmstadt on 1 December 2026. I will be supervised by Prof. Dr. Iryna Gurevych and co-supervised by Prof. Valentin Hofmann.

My research interests lie in natural language processing and machine learning, with a particular focus on low-resource NLP, lexical semantics, representation learning, and language models. My previous research includes work on distributional thesauri, contextualized representations, hypernymy detection, semantic resources, and neural models for biological sequence prediction.

My industry experience includes work at Oracle on AI systems for translating natural-language requests into declarative queries, including SQL, and on the Essbase calculation engine. I completed an integrated Bachelor and Master of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Bangalore, where my master’s thesis explored enriching word representations using distributional thesauri.
